Workflow

  1. Creative stages
  2. 01

    Briefing

    Review of the project, goals, references and product features.

  3. 02

    Concept & Script

    Development of the concept, script and cinematic language.

  4. 03

    Storyboard

    Planning of shots, framing, composition and aesthetics.

  5. Production stages
  6. 04

    Assets & LoRA

    Preparing references and, when needed, training dedicated LoRAs to preserve the products' visual identity.

  7. 05

    Image generation

    Generating keyframes and refining composition, lighting and consistency.

  8. 06

    Video generation

    Animating shots, camera moves, actions and transitions.

  9. 07

    Post-production

    Editing, visual effects, color grading, sound design and score.

  10. 08

    Finishing

    Technical and creative review, adjustments and export of final formats.
